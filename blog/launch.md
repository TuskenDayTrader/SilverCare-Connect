# Introducing SilverCare Connect: Bringing Families Closer, One Session at a Time

*Published: April 2026 | SilverCare Connect Team*

---

We're thrilled to announce the official launch of **SilverCare Connect** — a free session scheduling platform that makes it easy for families to arrange supported video visits with their loved ones at assisted living facilities.

If you've ever felt the quiet ache of distance from an aging parent or grandparent — or struggled to coordinate a video call that actually worked — this platform was built for you.

---

## The Problem We're Solving

Millions of families across the United States live far from aging loved ones in assisted living facilities. Visiting in person is expensive, logistically challenging, and sometimes impossible.

Technology promises to help — but for many seniors, it creates more barriers than it removes. Setting up a video call alone is confusing. Tablets and smartphones are unfamiliar. Wi-Fi connections are unreliable. And even when family members are ready and waiting, the call never quite happens.

Meanwhile, facility staff are stretched thin. Coordinating one-off video calls on top of daily care responsibilities isn't sustainable.

The result: **too many seniors go too long without seeing the faces of the people they love most.**

---

## Our Solution

SilverCare Connect is a **coordination layer** between families and facilities.

Here's how it works:

1. **A family member fills out a simple session request form** — no account needed, no app to download.
2. **SilverCare Connect notifies the facility** with all the session details in a structured, easy-to-act-on format.
3. **A staff member helps the resident get set up** before the call begins.
4. **The family joins the video session** — and the only thing they need to worry about is what to talk about.

We handle the coordination. The family just shows up.

---

## What Makes SilverCare Connect Different

We didn't build another video call app. We built a **scheduling and coordination service** designed specifically for the assisted living context.

- **No technical burden on residents.** Staff-assisted setup means residents don't have to figure anything out.
- **No confusing interfaces for families.** One clean form. One submit button. Done.
- **No cost during our pilot.** We're focused on proving the model and building trust before anything else.
- **Accessibility by design.** Large fonts, high contrast mode, multilingual support (English, Spanish, Chinese) — because families come in every form.

---

## Our Pilot: Pinellas County, Florida

We're launching our pilot in **Pinellas County, Florida**, home to one of the highest concentrations of assisted living facilities in the United States.

During the pilot:

- Sessions are **30 minutes** long
- Available **Monday through Wednesday**, **8:00 AM – 8:00 PM** local time
- Covering facilities within a **~20-mile radius** across Pinellas County
- **Completely free** for families and facilities alike

Families can search for their loved one's facility by ZIP code directly on our homepage.

## A Note to Facilities

SilverCare Connect was designed with your staff in mind. We know that any new program has to actually reduce workload — not add to it.

That's why our session requests come structured and complete: family name, resident name, date, time, and contact information. No back-and-forth. No ambiguity.
