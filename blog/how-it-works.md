# How SilverCare Connect Works: A Complete Guide for Families and Facilities

*Published: April 2026 | SilverCare Connect Team*

---

We know that getting started with a new service can feel daunting — especially when it involves coordinating with a loved one's care facility. That's why we've put together this step-by-step guide to walk you through exactly how SilverCare Connect works, from your first visit to our site all the way through to your first video session.

---

## Part 1: For Families

### Step 1 — Visit the SilverCare Connect Homepage

Go to [our homepage](#) and you'll see a short session request form near the center of the page.

You don't need to create an account. You don't need to download anything. You just need a few minutes and some basic information about your loved one.

---

### Step 2 — Find Your Loved One's Facility

In the session request form, enter the **ZIP code** of your loved one's assisted living facility. Our platform will automatically search our database and display a list of matching facilities.

Select the correct facility from the dropdown. A preview card will appear confirming the facility's name and address — so you can double-check before you continue.

> **Don't see the facility?**
> We're currently in our pilot phase and only cover Pinellas County, FL. If a facility isn't listed, it may not yet be in our network. [Contact us](#) to request it be added.

---

### Step 3 — Fill Out the Session Request Form

You'll need to provide:

| Field | What to Enter |
|---|---|
| **Your Name** | The name of the family member requesting the session |
| **Contact Email** | Your email address — we'll use this to confirm the session |
| **Resident Name** | Your loved one's name as it appears at the facility |
| **Preferred Date** | A Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday during the pilot |
| **Preferred Time** | Any time between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM local time |
| **Notes (optional)** | Anything the facility should know — e.g., "Grandma prefers mornings" or "Please use the common room TV" |

---

### Step 4 — Submit Your Request

Click **"Request Session"** to submit your form. You'll see a confirmation message on screen, and we'll send the details to the facility.

That's it on your end — for now.

---

### Step 5 — The Facility Confirms

After we send your request to the facility, a staff coordinator will review the details and confirm the session. You'll receive a confirmation email once everything is set.

*Note: Automated email confirmations are in active development. During the early pilot, confirmations may arrive via a brief follow-up from our team.*

---

### Step 6 — Join the Video Session

On the day of your session, simply join the video call at the agreed-upon time. The facility staff will have your loved one set up and ready before the call begins.

Your loved one doesn't need to press any buttons or navigate any menus — the staff handles all of that.

---

### Tips for a Great Session

- **Test your camera and microphone** before the call — use your device's built-in settings or join a test call.
- **Find a quiet, well-lit spot** where your loved one can see and hear you clearly.
- **Keep it simple and warm.** You don't need an agenda — just being present means the world.
- **Consider bringing other family members** onto the call for a mini family reunion.
- **30 minutes goes fast.** Have a few conversation starters ready: recent photos, a funny story, a question about their week.

## Part 2: For Facilities

### How Requests Arrive

When a family submits a session request through SilverCare Connect, your facility receives a structured notification containing:

- Family member's name and contact email
- Resident's name
- Requested date and time
- Any special notes from the family

All the information you need to act — in one place, in one message.

---

### What Facilities Are Expected to Do

1. **Review the incoming session request** and confirm whether the date/time works.
2. **Notify the resident's care team** so they can plan around the session.
3. **Prepare a device** (tablet, phone, or computer with camera) and a private or semi-private space for the call.
4. **Have a staff member available** during the session to assist the resident with setup and to be nearby if needed.
5. **Send a confirmation** back to SilverCare Connect so the family can be notified.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions for Facilities

**Do we need to install any software?**
No. You can use any video call platform your facility is already comfortable with (FaceTime, Zoom, Google Meet, etc.). SilverCare Connect is a scheduling coordination service — not a video platform.

**What if we need to reschedule?**
Contact the family directly using the email address provided in the session request, and let SilverCare Connect know so we can update our records.

**How much staff time does this require?**
Most sessions require 5–10 minutes of staff time for setup. The 30-minute session itself is resident and family time — staff can be nearby but don't need to participate.

**Is there any cost to participate?**
No. The pilot is completely free for facilities.

## Part 3: The Technology Behind It

You don't need to understand the tech to use SilverCare Connect — but for those who are curious, here's a brief overview.

### Session Request Flow

1. The family submits the request form on our homepage.
2. The form data is sent to our secure API server.
3. The facility is notified (currently via coordinated outreach; automated facility notifications are in development).
4. Session records are stored securely and accessible only to authorized users.

### Accessibility Features

Our platform is built with accessibility as a core requirement, not an afterthought:

- **Font size controls** (A− / A / A+) let users adjust text to their preferred reading size.
- **High contrast mode** improves visibility for users with low vision.
- **Reduced motion mode** minimizes animations for users with vestibular disorders.
- **Screen reader support** with ARIA labels, landmark regions, and a skip-to-content link.
- **Keyboard navigability** across all interactive elements.
- **Multilingual support** in English, Español, and 中文.

### Privacy & Data

- Session request data is collected only for the purpose of coordinating the session.
- No payment data is collected.
- Data is not sold or shared with third-party advertisers.
- All communications are handled over encrypted connections.
