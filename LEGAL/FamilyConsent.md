# Family Participation Consent

**SilverCare Connect — Supported Video Session Program**  
Version 1.0 | Effective Date: April 15, 2026

---

## Purpose of This Document

This consent document explains what SilverCare Connect's supported video session program involves, how your loved one's participation is facilitated, and what you and SilverCare Connect each agree to. Please read it carefully before submitting a session request.

---

## About the Program

SilverCare Connect is a pilot program that schedules **30-minute, staff-supported video calls** between residents of participating assisted living facilities and their family members. A trained SilverCare staff member or facility volunteer assists the resident with the technology during each session so families do not need to worry about technical barriers.

**Current pilot parameters:**
- Sessions are 30 minutes in length.
- Available Monday through Wednesday, 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM (Eastern Time).
- Service area: Pinellas County, FL (~20-mile radius).
- No charge during the pilot period.

---

## Family Member Agreement

By submitting a session request through SilverCare Connect, you ("the Family Member") agree to the following:

### 1. Accuracy of Information

You confirm that the information you provide — including the resident's name, the selected facility, and your contact details — is accurate to the best of your knowledge. You understand that inaccurate information may prevent the session from being scheduled.

### 2. Resident Wellbeing

You acknowledge that:
- Participation in video sessions is always **voluntary for the resident**.
- SilverCare staff and facility personnel will not force or coerce a resident to participate.
- If the resident is unable or unwilling to participate on the scheduled date (due to health, mood, or other circumstances), the session may be rescheduled or cancelled.

### 3. Session Conduct

You agree to:
- Conduct yourself respectfully toward facility staff, SilverCare staff, and the resident throughout the session.
- Not record the video session without the **explicit verbal consent** of all participants (including facility staff) at the time of recording.
- Not share images or recordings of the resident on social media or other public platforms without the resident's (or their legal guardian's) written consent.

### 4. Cancellation

If you need to cancel or reschedule, you agree to notify SilverCare Connect at least **24 hours before** the scheduled session so the timeslot can be made available to other families.

### 5. Limitation of Liability

You understand that SilverCare Connect acts as a scheduling and coordination intermediary. We are not a medical provider, care facility, or emergency service. We are not liable for:
- A session being cancelled due to resident health or facility circumstances.
- Technical issues with video platforms outside our control.
- The quality of care the resident receives at their facility.

## SilverCare Connect Commitments

SilverCare Connect agrees to:

1. **Handle your personal information** in accordance with our [Privacy Policy](PrivacyPolicy.md).
2. **Confirm, modify, or decline** your session request via email within **2 business days**.
3. **Send a reminder** 24 hours before the session.
4. **Assign a trained support person** to be present during the session to assist the resident with technology.
5. **Treat all participants** — residents, family members, and facility staff — with dignity and respect.
6. **Not share** your contact information or the resident's name with parties outside the facility involved in the session, except as required by law.

---

## Facility Partner Role

The participating assisted living facility:
- Agrees to allow SilverCare sessions on its premises.
- Provides a quiet, private space for the video call.
- May assign a staff member to assist with facilitation.
- Retains full authority over resident participation decisions.

SilverCare Connect is not an employee, agent, or representative of any facility. Facilities are independent partners.

---

## Special Considerations

### Residents with Dementia or Cognitive Impairment

For residents who may have reduced capacity to consent to participation, family members requesting sessions should:
- Have appropriate legal authority (power of attorney, healthcare surrogate, or equivalent).
- Discuss participation with the resident's primary care team and facility social worker before scheduling.
- Understand that facility staff may decline a session on any given day if participation does not appear to be in the resident's best interest.

### Emergency Situations

SilverCare Connect video sessions are not monitored for medical emergencies. If you observe concerning behavior or a potential emergency during a session, immediately contact the facility's front desk or call 911.
