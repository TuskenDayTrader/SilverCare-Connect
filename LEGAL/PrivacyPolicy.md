## 1. Introduction

SilverCare Connect ("we," "us," or "our") operates the SilverCare Connect platform (the "Service"), which helps families schedule supported video sessions with loved ones residing in assisted living facilities. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

| Data | How It Is Collected | Why We Collect It |
|------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Your name | Session request form | To identify the family contact |
| Email address | Session request form | To send booking confirmations and reminders |
| Resident's name | Session request form | To coordinate with facility staff |
| Preferred date and time | Session request form | To schedule the session |
| Selected facility | Session request form | To route the request to the correct facility |
| Optional notes | Session request form | To accommodate special needs or preferences |
| Account credentials (name, email, hashed password) | Registration (API) | To authenticate returning users |

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Local storage data** — When you use the demo mode (no backend), session request data is stored in your browser's `localStorage`. This data never leaves your device.
- **Server logs** — Our backend server may log IP addresses, request timestamps, and HTTP response codes for debugging and security purposes.
- **Cookies** — We do not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

### 2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

- We do not collect payment information (no payments are processed during the pilot).
- We do not collect sensitive medical or health information about residents.
- We do not use third-party advertising networks or sell data to advertisers.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. **Schedule and confirm video sessions** between families and residents.
2. **Send transactional communications** — booking confirmations, reminders, and cancellation notices via email.
3. **Improve the Service** — analyze aggregate (non-identifiable) usage patterns to understand how families use the platform.
4. **Communicate with you** — respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
5. **Ensure security** — detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

We will not use your personal information for marketing or promotional purposes without your explicit consent.

---

## 4. How We Share Your Information

### 4.1 With Facility Partners

When you submit a session request for a specific facility, we share the following with authorized staff at that facility:
- Your name and contact email
- Resident's name
- Requested date, time, and any notes

Facilities are contractually required to treat this information confidentially and use it only to coordinate your session.

### 4.2 With Service Providers

We may engage third-party providers to help operate the Service (e.g., email delivery services such as SendGrid, cloud hosting). These providers receive only the minimum data necessary and are bound by data processing agreements.

### 4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

### 4.4 We Do Not Sell Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

## 5. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Session request records | 12 months after the session date, then deleted |
| Account data | Until account deletion is requested |
| Server logs | 30 days rolling |
| localStorage demo data | Until you clear your browser data |

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see Section 10).

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 7. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

- Passwords stored using bcrypt hashing (never in plain text).
- API access protected by signed JWT tokens.
- HTTPS required for all communications between browsers and our servers.
- Environment variables used for all secrets (never hardcoded in source code).

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your information.
