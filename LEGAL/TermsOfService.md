## 5. Session Requests

### 5.1 Requesting a Session

Submitting a session request does not guarantee a confirmed booking. Requests are subject to:
- Facility availability on the requested date and time.
- Confirmation from facility staff.
- Resident's wellbeing and willingness to participate.

We will notify you by email once your request is confirmed, modified, or declined.

### 5.2 Cancellations

If you need to cancel or reschedule a session, please notify us at least **24 hours in advance** so the timeslot can be made available to other families.
