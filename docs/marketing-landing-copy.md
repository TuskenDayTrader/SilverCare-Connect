## Value Proposition Block

> *"We handle the tech. You just show up."*

Millions of families live miles — or states — away from aging parents and grandparents. Visiting in person isn't always possible, but connection is. SilverCare Connect bridges that gap by coordinating video sessions directly with assisted living facilities, so your loved one is ready and supported before the call even begins.

**No app downloads. No confusing logins. Just a warm face on the screen.**

## Feature Highlights

### ✅ Hassle-Free Setup
Our team coordinates directly with the facility so your loved one doesn't have to figure out technology on their own. Staff are notified, devices are ready, and the call happens on time.

### 📅 Simple Scheduling
Request a 30-minute session in minutes. Choose a date (Monday–Wednesday), pick a time between 8 AM and 8 PM, and we'll handle the rest.

### 🏠 Pinellas County Pilot
We're currently serving assisted living facilities across Pinellas County, Florida. Search by ZIP code to find your loved one's facility and see available session times.

### 🔒 Private & Secure
Session details are kept confidential. We never share your personal information with third parties, and all data is handled with care.

### 🌐 Multilingual Support
Our platform supports English, Español, and 中文 — because families come in every language.

### ♿ Accessibility First
Large font options, high-contrast mode, and screen-reader compatibility ensure the platform works for everyone in your family.

## How It Works Section

**Three simple steps to your next video visit:**

1. **Find Your Facility**
   Enter your loved one's ZIP code to find their assisted living facility in our network.

2. **Request Your Session**
   Fill out a short form with your name, contact email, your loved one's name, your preferred date and time, and any special notes.

3. **We Take It From There**
   SilverCare Connect notifies the facility, confirms the session, and ensures your loved one is ready for the call.
