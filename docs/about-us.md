# About SilverCare Connect

---

## Our Mission

SilverCare Connect exists to close the distance between families and their aging loved ones.

We believe that connection is a form of care — and that no senior should feel isolated simply because geography, schedules, or technology stands in the way of a visit. Our platform makes it easy to schedule a supported video session with a loved one at their assisted living facility, so families can see each other face-to-face even when they can't be there in person.

**Our mission:** Make meaningful family connection simple, accessible, and dependable for every senior and their family — regardless of distance or technical ability.

---

## Our Vision

We envision a future where every senior in every assisted living facility has consistent, joyful contact with the people who love them most. Where a grandson in Seattle can share lunch with his grandmother in St. Petersburg. Where a daughter in Miami can wish her father a happy birthday — with a smile, not just a text message.

SilverCare Connect is the bridge that makes that possible.

---

## Our Story

The idea for SilverCare Connect was born out of a deeply personal experience shared by many families: the quiet frustration of wanting to visit more, but not being able to.

When a loved one moves into assisted living, families often struggle to stay connected. Phone calls get missed. Video apps confuse residents. Facility staff are stretched thin. And the result — too often — is that visits become rare, and seniors feel increasingly alone.

We asked ourselves: *What if scheduling a video session were as simple as requesting an appointment?* What if the technology didn't stand in the way?

From that question, SilverCare Connect was created — a coordination layer between families and facilities that handles the logistics, so families and seniors can simply focus on each other.

We launched our pilot in Pinellas County, Florida, working with local assisted living facilities to test and refine the experience. Our goal is to expand to every county in Florida and, eventually, across the country.

---

## What We Do

SilverCare Connect is a session scheduling platform for families and assisted living facilities.

- **Families** use our platform to request a 30-minute video session with their loved one.
- **Facilities** receive structured, clear session requests that reduce coordination burden on staff.
- **Residents** get a confirmed, staff-supported video visit — no app downloads or tech setup required on their end.

We currently serve **Pinellas County, FL** during our pilot phase, with plans to expand.

## Our Values

### 💛 Connection First
Every feature we build starts with one question: does this make it easier for families to connect?

### 🤝 Dignity & Respect
We design for seniors, not around them. Every part of our experience is built with accessibility and ease of use in mind.

### 🔒 Privacy & Trust
We collect only the data we need, protect it carefully, and never sell it. Families trust us with something precious — we don't take that lightly.

### 🌐 Inclusivity
Our platform supports multiple languages and accessibility features because families come in every form.

### 🚀 Simplicity
We believe the best technology is invisible. If it takes more than three steps, we've made it too complicated.
