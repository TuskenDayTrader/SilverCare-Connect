## Getting Started

### Q1: What is SilverCare Connect?

**A:** SilverCare Connect is a free session scheduling service that helps families arrange supported video visits with their loved ones at assisted living facilities. We coordinate directly with the facility so that a staff member can help your loved one get set up for the call — no technical know-how required on their end. During the current pilot, we cover facilities in Pinellas County, FL.

---

### Q2: Who can use SilverCare Connect?

**A:** SilverCare Connect is designed for two groups of users:

- **Families** — family members or close friends who want to schedule a video session with a resident at a participating facility.
- **Facility staff** — staff at participating assisted living facilities who review session requests, coordinate with residents, and assist with the technology on the facility side.

Anyone in a participating facility's service area can request a session. You do not need to be a technical expert.

---

### Q3: How do I get started as a family member?

**A:** Getting started is simple:

1. Visit the SilverCare Connect homepage.
2. Enter your loved one's ZIP code to find their facility.
3. Select the facility from the dropdown.
4. Enter your name, contact email, your loved one's name, your preferred date and time, and any special notes.
5. Submit the form — we'll take it from there.

You will receive a confirmation email once your session is reviewed and approved by facility staff. You do not need to create an account to submit a session request during the pilot phase.

---

### Q4: Is SilverCare Connect free to use?

**A:** Yes. During our current pilot phase, all session requests are **completely free**. No payment information is collected or required.

---

### Q5: Which facilities are currently supported?

**A:** During the pilot, SilverCare Connect is available at select assisted living facilities in **Pinellas County, FL** (approximately a 20-mile radius). You can find a map of participating facilities via the [BatchGeo pilot map](https://www.batchgeo.com/map/0bab8e4477dc55548d1a79dc440e8ab2).

To check whether a specific facility is participating, enter your ZIP code in the session request form — only facilities in the pilot area will appear in the dropdown.

If your loved one's facility isn't listed, you can:
- Check back as we add more facilities over time.
- [Contact us](#) to request that a specific facility be added.
- Ask the facility's management to [become a SilverCare Connect partner](#).

---

### Q6: Can I request sessions in languages other than English?

**A:** Yes. Our platform supports **English, Español (Spanish), and 中文 (Chinese)**. Use the language toggle at the top of the page to switch languages before filling out the form.

## Session Scheduling

### Q7: When are sessions available?

**A:** During the pilot, sessions are available:

- **Days:** Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday only
- **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM (local time)
- **Length:** 30 minutes per session

Sessions outside these windows cannot be booked through the current system.

---

### Q8: How far in advance do I need to request a session?

**A:** We recommend submitting your request at least **24–48 hours** before your desired session time to give facility staff enough time to prepare and notify your loved one. Same-day requests may not always be accommodated.

> **⚠️ Flag for product team:** A formal advance-notice policy has not yet been defined. Please confirm and update this answer before launch.

---

### Q9: How will I know if my session request was approved?

**A:** After submitting your request, you will receive an email acknowledgment. Once facility staff review and approve your request, you will receive a second confirmation email with session details. If you do not receive a confirmation within 48 hours, check your Spam/Junk folder or contact the facility directly.

---

### Q10: What if I need to cancel or reschedule a session?

**A:** To cancel or reschedule, please contact the facility staff directly using the contact information provided in your confirmation email. We recommend giving at least 24 hours' notice so staff can update their schedule.

> **⚠️ Flag for product team:** A self-service cancel/reschedule feature is not yet available. Please confirm the intended process and update this answer.

---

### Q11: Can I request sessions for multiple residents or facilities?

**A:** Yes. Each session request is submitted separately. There is no limit on the number of sessions you can request, though session availability depends on the facility's schedule and staff capacity. If you have a loved one at more than one facility, simply submit a request for each one individually.

## Technical & Troubleshooting

### Q12: What do I need to join a video session?

**A:** To join a video session, you need:

- A device with a **camera and microphone** (laptop, desktop, tablet, or smartphone)
- A **stable internet connection** (Wi-Fi or wired recommended)
- A **supported browser** — Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge work best
- **Camera and microphone permissions** enabled in your browser

No special software or app download is required. Your loved one at the facility does not need to do anything on their own — facility staff will assist with setup on their end.

---

### Q13: The video/audio isn't working during my session. What should I do?

**A:** Here are the most common quick fixes:

1. Make sure you clicked **Allow** when your browser asked for camera/microphone access.
2. Close any other apps using your camera (e.g., Zoom, FaceTime).
3. Refresh the page and try again.
4. Try a different browser — Google Chrome is recommended.
5. Restart your device and rejoin.

For a full step-by-step guide, see the [Support & Troubleshooting Guide](support.md).

---

### Q14: I submitted a session request form but got an error. What went wrong?

**A:** The most common reasons a session request form fails:

- **Missing required fields** — All required fields must be filled in. Scroll up to find any highlighted fields.
- **Invalid ZIP code** — Only 5-digit US ZIP codes within the Pinellas County pilot area are accepted.
- **Invalid date** — Sessions are only available Monday–Wednesday. Selecting a different day will cause an error.
- **No facilities listed** — If no facilities appear in the dropdown after you enter your ZIP, the area may not be covered by the pilot yet.

---

### Q15: Does SilverCare Connect work on mobile devices?

**A:** Yes. The SilverCare Connect website is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. Accessibility features including font size adjustment and high-contrast mode are available on all devices.

Supported mobile browsers include **Chrome for Android**, **Safari for iOS** (version 14+), and **Firefox for Android**. For the best experience during video sessions, we recommend using a **tablet or laptop** rather than a small phone screen.

---

### Q16: What browsers are supported?

**A:** SilverCare Connect works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge. We recommend keeping your browser updated for the best experience.

## Privacy & Security

### Q17: Is my information kept private?

**A:** Yes. SilverCare Connect takes privacy seriously. Here is what you should know:

- Your personal information (name, email, contact details) is used only to schedule and confirm your session.
- Information about your loved one (resident name, facility) is shared only with the staff at their facility.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.
- Video sessions are not recorded by SilverCare Connect.

---

### Q18: Is it safe to enter my email address and contact information on the form?

**A:** Yes. The SilverCare Connect website uses HTTPS (encrypted connections) to protect any information you enter. You will see a padlock icon in your browser's address bar when visiting the site.

Only the information needed to arrange your session is collected — we do not ask for payment details, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal information.

---

### Q19: Is my session request data stored securely?

**A:** Yes. Session request data is stored securely and access is limited to authorized SilverCare Connect staff and the relevant facility. We follow industry-standard security practices.

---

### Q20: Will my video session be recorded?

**A:** No. SilverCare Connect does not record video sessions. Sessions are live, private calls between family members and their loved ones at the facility.

### Q22: How does SilverCare Connect benefit our facility?

**A:** SilverCare Connect reduces the ad-hoc coordination burden on your staff by delivering structured, clear session requests with all the information you need — resident name, date, time, and family contact details. It also helps improve resident well-being by increasing the frequency of meaningful family visits. No special software needs to be installed.

---

### Q23: What is the role of facility staff in a session?

**A:** Facility staff play an important role in making sessions successful:

- **Review and approve** incoming session requests from families.
- **Notify the resident** in advance of their scheduled session.
- **Set up the device** (charged tablet or computer, connected to facility Wi-Fi) in a comfortable, well-lit location before the session time.
- **Assist the resident** in joining the call if needed.
- **Follow up** with families if a session needs to be rescheduled.

---

### Q24: What if a resident is unable to participate on the scheduled day?

**A:** If a resident is unable to participate (e.g., due to illness or a facility activity conflict), please contact the family member using the email address in the session request as soon as possible so they can reschedule.

### Q25: Is there a cost for facilities to participate?

**A:** No. Participation in the SilverCare Connect pilot is **free for facilities**. We're focused on building a great experience during our pilot phase before introducing any premium features.
