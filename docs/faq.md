## Getting Started

### Q1: What is SilverCare Connect?

**A:** SilverCare Connect is a service that makes it easy for families to schedule supported video sessions with their loved ones at participating assisted living facilities. We handle the technology so you can focus on simply showing up and connecting. During the current pilot, we cover facilities in Pinellas County, FL.

---

### Q2: Who can use SilverCare Connect?

**A:** SilverCare Connect is designed for two groups of users:

- **Families** — family members or close friends who want to schedule a video session with a resident at a participating facility.
- **Facility staff** — staff at participating assisted living facilities who review session requests, coordinate with residents, and assist with the technology on the facility side.

Anyone in a participating facility's service area can request a session. You do not need to be a technical expert.

---

### Q3: How do I get started as a family member?

**A:** Getting started is simple:

1. Visit the SilverCare Connect homepage.
2. Scroll to the **"Request a Session"** form or click the button in the navigation.
3. Fill in your contact details, your loved one's name, the facility, and your preferred date and time.
4. Submit the form. You will receive a confirmation email once your session is reviewed and approved by facility staff.

You do not need to create an account to submit a session request during the pilot phase.

---

### Q4: Is SilverCare Connect free to use?

**A:** Yes. SilverCare Connect is currently operating as a pilot program. No payments are collected at this time. Session requests are free to submit.

---

### Q5: Which facilities are currently supported?

**A:** During the pilot, SilverCare Connect is available at select assisted living facilities in **Pinellas County, FL** (approximately a 20-mile radius). You can find a map of participating facilities via the [BatchGeo pilot map](https://www.batchgeo.com/map/0bab8e4477dc55548d1a79dc440e8ab2).

To check whether a specific facility is participating, enter your ZIP code in the session request form — only facilities in the pilot area will appear in the dropdown.

## Session Scheduling

### Q6: When are sessions available?

**A:** During the pilot, sessions are available:

- **Days:** Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday only
- **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM (local time)
- **Length:** 30 minutes per session

Sessions outside these windows cannot be booked through the current system.

---

### Q7: How far in advance do I need to request a session?

**A:** We recommend submitting your request at least **24–48 hours** before your desired session time to give facility staff enough time to prepare and notify your loved one. Same-day requests may not always be accommodated.

> **⚠️ Flag for product team:** A formal advance-notice policy has not yet been defined. Please confirm and update this answer before launch.

---

### Q8: How will I know if my session request was approved?

**A:** After submitting your request, you will receive an email acknowledgment. Once facility staff review and approve your request, you will receive a second confirmation email with session details. If you do not receive a confirmation within 48 hours, check your Spam/Junk folder or contact the facility directly.

---

### Q9: Can I cancel or change a session request?

**A:** To cancel or modify a session request, contact the facility staff directly. Use the contact information provided in your confirmation email or visit the facility's page on SilverCare Connect.

> **⚠️ Flag for product team:** A self-service cancel/reschedule feature is not yet available. Please confirm the intended process and update this answer.

---

### Q10: Can I request multiple sessions?

**A:** Yes. You can submit a new session request for each visit. There is no limit on the number of sessions you can request, though session availability depends on the facility's schedule and staff capacity.

## Technical & Troubleshooting

### Q11: What do I need to join a video session?

**A:** To join a video session, you need:

- A device with a **camera and microphone** (laptop, desktop, tablet, or smartphone)
- A **stable internet connection** (Wi-Fi or wired recommended)
- A **supported browser** — Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge work best (see [Browser & Device Compatibility Tips](support.md#6-browser--device-compatibility-tips))
- **Camera and microphone permissions** enabled in your browser

No special software or app download is required.

---

### Q12: The video/audio isn't working during my session. What should I do?

**A:** Here are the most common quick fixes:

1. Make sure you clicked **Allow** when your browser asked for camera/microphone access.
2. Close any other apps using your camera (e.g., Zoom, FaceTime).
3. Refresh the page and try again.
4. Try a different browser — Google Chrome is recommended.
5. Restart your device and rejoin.

For a full step-by-step guide, see [The Session Video / Call Won't Start](support.md#3-the-session-video--call-wont-start) in the Support Guide.

---

### Q13: I submitted a session request form but got an error. What went wrong?

**A:** The most common reasons a session request form fails:

- **Missing required fields** — All fields with a red asterisk (*) must be filled in. Scroll up to find any highlighted fields.
- **Invalid ZIP code** — Only 5-digit US ZIP codes within the Pinellas County pilot area are accepted.
- **Invalid date** — Sessions are only available Monday–Wednesday. Selecting a different day will cause an error.
- **No facilities listed** — If no facilities appear in the dropdown after you enter your ZIP, the area may not be covered by the pilot yet.

See [Problems Submitting a Session Request Form](support.md#5-problems-submitting-a-session-request-form) for a full table of error messages and solutions.

---

### Q14: Does SilverCare Connect work on my phone or tablet?

**A:** Yes. SilverCare Connect works on modern smartphones and tablets. For the best experience during video sessions, we recommend using a **tablet or laptop** rather than a small phone screen. Make sure your browser app is up to date.

Supported mobile browsers include **Chrome for Android**, **Safari for iOS** (version 14+), and **Firefox for Android**.

## Privacy & Security

### Q15: Is my information kept private?

**A:** Yes. SilverCare Connect takes privacy seriously. Here is what you should know:

- Your personal information (name, email, contact details) is used only to schedule and confirm your session.
- Information about your loved one (resident name, facility) is shared only with the staff at their facility.
- We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
- Video sessions are not recorded by SilverCare Connect.

> **⚠️ Flag for product team:** A formal privacy policy has not yet been published. Please add a link to the privacy policy here and confirm the data handling practices above before launch.

---

### Q16: Is it safe to enter my email address and contact information on the form?

**A:** Yes. The SilverCare Connect website uses HTTPS (encrypted connections) to protect any information you enter. You will see a padlock icon in your browser's address bar when visiting the site.

Only the information needed to arrange your session is collected — we do not ask for payment details, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal information.

---

### Q17: Will my video session be recorded?

**A:** No. SilverCare Connect does not record video sessions. Sessions are live, private calls between family members and their loved ones at the facility.

## For Facility Staff

### Q18: How does my facility sign up for SilverCare Connect?

**A:** During the pilot phase, facilities are enrolled by the SilverCare Connect team. If you are interested in participating, please have your facility manager or administrator reach out to the SilverCare Connect coordinator.

### Q19: What is the role of facility staff in a session?

**A:** Facility staff play an important role in making sessions successful:

- **Review and approve** incoming session requests from families.
- **Notify the resident** in advance of their scheduled session.
- **Set up the device** (charged tablet or computer, connected to facility Wi-Fi) in a comfortable, well-lit location before the session time.
- **Assist the resident** in joining the call if needed.
- **Follow up** with families if a session needs to be rescheduled.

---

### Q20: What if a resident is unable to participate on the scheduled day?

**A:** If a resident is unable to participate (e.g., due to illness or a facility activity conflict), please contact the family member using the email address in the session request as soon as possible so they can reschedule. You can also cancel the session on their behalf.
