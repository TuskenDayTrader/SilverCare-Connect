# Frequently Asked Questions

**SilverCare Connect** — *Connecting families and seniors through seamless session scheduling.*

---

## For Families

---

### 1. What is SilverCare Connect?

SilverCare Connect is a free session scheduling service that helps families arrange supported video visits with their loved ones at assisted living facilities. We coordinate directly with the facility so that a staff member can help your loved one get set up for the call — no technical know-how required on their end.

---

### 2. How do I request a session?

Simply fill out the session request form on our homepage:

1. Enter your loved one's ZIP code to find their facility.
2. Select the facility from the dropdown.
3. Enter your name, contact email, your loved one's name, your preferred date and time, and any special notes.
4. Submit the form — we'll take it from there.

Sessions are currently available **Monday through Wednesday**, between **8:00 AM and 8:00 PM** local time.

---

### 3. Is SilverCare Connect free to use?

Yes. During our current pilot phase, all session requests are **completely free**. No payment information is collected or required.

---

### 4. What happens after I submit a session request?

Once your request is received, we notify the facility with all the relevant details. The facility confirms the session and ensures a staff member is available to assist your loved one with the call. You'll receive a confirmation once the session is set.

---

### 5. What technology does my loved one need?

Your loved one doesn't need to do anything on their own. Facility staff will assist them with getting set up for the video call using the facility's available devices. You'll need a device (smartphone, tablet, or computer) with a camera and internet connection to join from your end.

---

### 6. What if my loved one's facility isn't listed?

We're currently in our pilot phase and serve assisted living facilities in **Pinellas County, Florida**. If your loved one's facility isn't listed, it may not yet be part of our network.

You can:
- Check back as we add more facilities over time.
- [Contact us](#) to request that a specific facility be added.
- Ask the facility's management to [become a SilverCare Connect partner](#).

---

### 7. Can I request sessions in languages other than English?

Yes. Our platform supports **English, Español (Spanish), and 中文 (Chinese)**. Use the language toggle at the top of the page to switch languages before filling out the form.

---

### 8. What if I need to cancel or reschedule a session?

To cancel or reschedule, please contact the facility directly as soon as possible. We recommend giving at least 24 hours' notice so staff can update their schedule.

*Note: A dedicated cancellation and rescheduling flow is planned for a future update.*

---

### 9. How long is each session?

Sessions are **30 minutes** long. If you need more time, you're welcome to request an additional session on a future date.

---

### 10. Can I request sessions for multiple residents or facilities?

Yes. Each session request is submitted separately. If you have a loved one at more than one facility, simply submit a request for each one individually.

---

## Privacy & Data

---

### 11. What personal information does SilverCare Connect collect?

When you submit a session request, we collect:

- Your name and contact email
- Your loved one's name
- The facility, date, time, and any notes you provide

We do not collect payment information, Social Security numbers, medical records, or any other sensitive personal data.

---

### 12. How is my information used?

Your information is used solely to coordinate the video session with the facility. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

### 13. Is my session request data stored securely?

Yes. Session request data is stored securely and access is limited to authorized SilverCare Connect staff and the relevant facility. We follow industry-standard security practices.

---

## For Facilities

---

### 14. How does SilverCare Connect benefit our facility?

SilverCare Connect reduces the ad-hoc coordination burden on your staff by delivering structured, clear session requests with all the information you need — resident name, date, time, and family contact details. It also helps improve resident well-being by increasing the frequency of meaningful family visits.

---

### 15. What does our facility need to participate?

- A device with a camera and internet connection (tablet, smartphone, or computer).
- A staff member available during session times to assist residents.
- Agreement to our facility partner guidelines.

No special software needs to be installed. Sessions can be conducted via widely available video call platforms.

---

### 16. Is there a cost for facilities to participate?

No. Participation in the SilverCare Connect pilot is **free for facilities**. We're focused on building a great experience during our pilot phase before introducing any premium features.

---

### 17. How does our facility get listed on SilverCare Connect?

Facilities in Pinellas County, FL can [contact us](#) or visit our [Facility Partner page](#) to express interest. We'll reach out to discuss onboarding and answer any questions.

---

## Technical Questions

---

### 18. Does SilverCare Connect work on mobile devices?

Yes. The SilverCare Connect website is fully responsive and works on smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers. Accessibility features including font size adjustment and high-contrast mode are available on all devices.

---

### 19. What browsers are supported?

SilverCare Connect works on all modern browsers including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge. We recommend keeping your browser updated for the best experience.
