## 1. I Can't Log In

**Symptoms:** The login page shows an error, you're redirected back to the login screen, or nothing happens when you click "Sign In."

**Step-by-step fixes:**

1. **Double-check your email address.** Make sure there are no extra spaces before or after it. Email addresses are case-insensitive, but typos are easy to make.
2. **Check your password.** Passwords *are* case-sensitive. Make sure Caps Lock is not accidentally turned on.
3. **Try resetting your password.** If you're unsure of your password, see [How Do I Reset My Password?](#4-how-do-i-reset-my-password) below.
4. **Clear your browser cache and cookies.**
   - Chrome / Edge: `Ctrl + Shift + Delete` (Windows) or `Cmd + Shift + Delete` (Mac) → select "Cookies and other site data" + "Cached images and files" → click "Clear data."
   - Safari: `Safari → Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All.`
   - Firefox: `Ctrl + Shift + Delete` → select "Cookies and Site Data" + "Cached Web Content" → click "Clear."
5. **Try a different browser** (see [Browser & Device Compatibility Tips](#6-browser--device-compatibility-tips)).
6. **Disable browser extensions** (especially ad-blockers or privacy extensions) and try again.
7. **Check for a service outage.** If multiple people at your facility are having the same issue at the same time, the service may be temporarily unavailable. Wait 10–15 minutes and try again.

If none of the above work, contact facility staff or your SilverCare Connect coordinator (see [Getting More Help](#getting-more-help)).

## 2. I Did Not Receive My Registration Email

**Symptoms:** You registered for an account but never received a confirmation or welcome email.

**Step-by-step fixes:**

1. **Wait a few minutes.** Emails can be delayed by up to 5–10 minutes depending on your email provider.
2. **Check your Spam / Junk folder.** Most email clients have a separate folder for messages flagged as spam. Look for an email from SilverCare Connect there.
3. **Check your Promotions or Updates tabs** (Gmail users). Gmail sometimes routes automated emails to these tabs instead of your main inbox.
4. **Verify you used the correct email address.** If you made a typo when registering, the email may have gone to a different address. Try registering again with the correct address.
5. **Add our email address to your contacts.** This prevents future emails from being flagged as spam. *(The exact sender address will be provided by your facility coordinator.)*
6. **Try a different email provider.** Some corporate or school email systems aggressively filter automated messages. A personal Gmail, Outlook, or Yahoo address is generally more reliable.
7. **Request a new confirmation email.** On the login page, look for a "Resend confirmation email" link and click it.

> **Still nothing?** Contact facility staff or your SilverCare Connect coordinator — they can manually verify your account or resend the email on your behalf.

## 3. The Session Video / Call Won't Start

**Symptoms:** You click to join your scheduled session but the video or audio doesn't work, the screen stays blank, or you see a connection error.

**Step-by-step fixes:**

1. **Check your internet connection.**
   - Try opening another website (e.g., google.com) to confirm you're connected.
   - If on Wi-Fi, move closer to your router or try switching to a wired connection.
   - Restart your router if other devices are also having connectivity issues.

2. **Allow camera and microphone permissions.**
   - Your browser will ask for camera/microphone access the first time you join a session. Click **Allow**.
   - If you accidentally clicked Block, you need to update your browser settings:
     - **Chrome:** Click the camera/lock icon in the address bar → Site Settings → Allow Camera and Microphone.
     - **Firefox:** Click the camera icon in the address bar → Allow.
     - **Safari:** Safari → Settings → Websites → Camera / Microphone → Allow for this site.

3. **Close other apps or tabs using the camera or microphone.** Only one application can use your camera at a time. Close video-call apps (Zoom, Teams, FaceTime) and other browser tabs that may be using your camera.

4. **Refresh the page.** Sometimes a simple page refresh (`F5` or `Cmd + R`) resolves a stalled connection.

5. **Restart your browser** completely (close all windows, then reopen).

6. **Try a different browser.** Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge tend to have the best video call support.

7. **Restart your device.** If all else fails, fully shut down your computer, tablet, or phone and turn it back on.

8. **Check whether the session is still within its scheduled window.** Sessions are 30 minutes long and are scheduled Monday–Wednesday, 8:00 AM–8:00 PM local time. Trying to connect outside this window will not work.

9. **Confirm the session was approved.** Session requests must be confirmed by facility staff before a call can take place. Check your email for a confirmation message.

> **Tip for facility staff:** Make sure the resident's shared tablet or device is charged, connected to the facility Wi-Fi, and positioned in a well-lit area before the session time.

## 4. How Do I Reset My Password?

**Step-by-step instructions:**

1. Go to the SilverCare Connect login page.
2. Click **"Forgot password?"** (located below the Sign In button).
3. Enter the email address you used to register and click **"Send Reset Link."**
4. Check your inbox (and Spam/Junk folder) for an email with the subject line containing "Reset Your Password."
5. Click the reset link inside the email. *(This link is only valid for 1 hour.)*
6. Enter a new password — choose something at least 8 characters long with a mix of letters and numbers.
7. Confirm your new password and click **"Save."**
8. You will be redirected to the login page. Sign in with your new password.

> **Did not receive the reset email?** Follow the same steps as [I Did Not Receive My Registration Email](#2-i-did-not-receive-my-registration-email).

> **Link expired?** Simply go back to the "Forgot password?" page and request a new link.

## 5. Problems Submitting a Session Request Form

**Symptoms:** The form won't submit, error messages appear on the form, or the page reloads without a success message.

**Common errors and how to fix them:**

| Error Message | What to Do |
|---|---|
| "Please fill out all required fields" | Scroll up — fields with a red border are missing information. Fill them all in. |
| "Please enter a valid email address" | Make sure your email follows the format `you@example.com` with no spaces. |
| "Please enter a valid ZIP code" | Enter a 5-digit US ZIP code (e.g., `33707`). The form only works for Pinellas County, FL during the pilot. |
| "No facilities found for this ZIP code" | Only facilities in the Pinellas County pilot area are listed. Contact your facility to confirm it is participating. |
| "Please select a valid session date" | Sessions are only available Monday–Wednesday. Select a date that falls on one of those days. |
| "Please select a time" | Click the time dropdown and choose one of the available slots. |

**General tips:**

- **Use a supported browser.** Older browsers may not render the form correctly. See [Browser & Device Compatibility Tips](#6-browser--device-compatibility-tips).
- **Disable auto-fill conflicts.** Some browser auto-fill tools insert incorrect data. Try filling the form manually.
- **Check your internet connection.** The form needs an active internet connection to submit.
- **Try the form again after a few minutes.** If the server was temporarily unavailable, a short wait often fixes it.
- **Refresh the page if the form seems frozen.** Your filled-in data will be lost, but the form will be ready to use again.

> **Note for families:** During the pilot phase, session requests are reviewed and confirmed by facility staff. You will receive a confirmation email once your session is approved.

## 6. Browser & Device Compatibility Tips

SilverCare Connect is designed to work on modern browsers and devices. Follow the tips below for the best experience.

### Recommended Browsers

| Browser | Minimum Version | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Google Chrome | Version 90+ | **Best overall experience** |
| Microsoft Edge | Version 90+ | Works well; built on Chrome |
| Mozilla Firefox | Version 88+ | Good compatibility |
| Safari | Version 14+ (macOS/iOS) | Works on Apple devices |

> **Avoid:** Internet Explorer (any version) — it is no longer supported and will not work correctly.

### Recommended Devices

- **Desktop or laptop computers** (Windows, Mac, or Linux) — ideal for video sessions due to screen size and stable connections.
- **iPads and Android tablets** — work well for video sessions; make sure the browser app is up to date.
- **Smartphones (iPhone / Android)** — supported, though a larger screen is recommended for video calls.

### Keep Your Browser Up to Date

An outdated browser is the most common cause of unexpected issues.

- **Chrome:** Click the three dots (⋮) in the top-right → Help → About Google Chrome. Updates install automatically.
- **Edge:** Click the three dots (⋯) → Help and feedback → About Microsoft Edge.
- **Firefox:** Click the three lines (☰) → Help → About Firefox.
- **Safari:** Update via System Preferences → Software Update (Mac) or Settings → General → Software Update (iOS).

### General Tips

- **Use a stable internet connection.** A wired connection or strong Wi-Fi is recommended for video sessions. Avoid joining from public Wi-Fi hotspots if possible.
- **Close unused tabs and apps.** Fewer background apps means better video/audio quality.
- **Enable JavaScript.** SilverCare Connect requires JavaScript to be enabled in your browser (it is on by default in all modern browsers).
- **Accessibility features.** SilverCare Connect supports larger text sizes, high-contrast mode, and reduced motion. Look for the accessibility bar at the top of the page.
- **Language support.** The site is available in English, Español, and 中文. Use the language switcher at the top of the page.
