I hope this message finds you well. I'm reaching out to share some exciting news that I think could make a real difference for the families and seniors you serve.

We've just launched **SilverCare Connect** — a free, easy-to-use platform that helps families schedule supported video sessions with their loved ones at assisted living facilities in Pinellas County, Florida.

---

**The problem we're solving:**

Millions of families live far from aging parents and grandparents in assisted living. Technology is supposed to help — but setting up video calls is often confusing for seniors, and coordinating with busy facility staff adds to the frustration. Too many meaningful visits just don't happen.

---

**How SilverCare Connect helps:**

- Families submit a simple session request online — no account or app required
- We notify the facility with all the session details in a clear, structured format
- Staff help the resident get set up before the call begins
- The family just shows up — we handle everything else

Sessions are 30 minutes, available Monday–Wednesday, 8 AM–8 PM local time.
**Completely free** during our pilot phase.

---

*For press / media:* SilverCare Connect is a new approach to a growing problem — senior isolation in assisted living facilities. We'd be happy to provide more information, arrange an interview, or connect you with families willing to share their stories.
