Facility Partner Overview

SilverCare Connect is a pilot service that helps families stay connected with residents at assisted living facilities through short, scheduled video sessions — with on-site tech support included.

We handle the technology. Your staff stays focused on care.

How It Works

From Request to Connection

  1. 1

    A Family Requests

    A family member requests a session through our website — choosing a preferred date and time.

  2. 2

    We Coordinate

    We confirm with your facility to ensure the time fits your schedule and routines.

  3. 3

    We Arrive On-Site

    We set up the tech and support a 30-minute Family Connect Session. No equipment burden on your staff. No disruption to daily programming.

Requirements

What We Need from Your Facility

  • A quiet space with Wi-Fi access (resident room or common area)
  • A brief coordination contact (phone/email) to confirm session times
  • ~5 minutes of staff awareness per session (not staff time)

Benefits

Why Partner with Us?

  • Family satisfaction. Reliable connection reduces complaints and increases family trust.
  • Zero staff burden. We bring the tech and manage the session end-to-end.
  • Resident well-being. Consistent family contact is linked to reduced isolation and improved mood.
  • Accessibility built in. Our platform supports multiple languages, high contrast, large fonts, and screen readers.
